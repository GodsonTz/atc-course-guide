About This Guide

Why We Built This

We're three Electrical Engineering students at Arusha Technical College. Every year we watched new students pick a course the same way we once did, by guessing, by following a friend, or by taking whatever seat was open. Most never saw a full list of programmes, what they actually involve, or where they lead.

So we built the guide we wished we'd had: every ATC programme in one place, in plain English and Kiswahili, with a short quiz to help you match your interests to a real course instead of a rumour.
